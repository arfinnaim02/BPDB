# Monthly production-cost experiment v1

Registered 8 November 2026 before this experiment's cost forecasts or errors are generated.
Target: sum of national daily total_production_cost (BDT) for a complete calendar month, using validated canonical Page 1 originals. This is reported production cost, not market electricity price or a full audited financial expenditure measure. Independent accounting-scope review remains pending; outputs are exploratory research.

Window: 2025-01-01 through last fully ended calendar month at capture. Future target: next calendar month relative to actual capture in Asia/Dhaka. Partial current months never become training labels. Preserve every calendar day and month, all source/version identities and missing/held flags. A target month requires every original daily cost, correct BDT/daily/all/national dimensions, linked source/page/checksum, finite positive cost and no existing physical-review flag. No imputation, monthly extrapolation or suspect-value repair. Preserve exclusion reasons.

Historical issue: day 8 of the preceding month, noon Asia/Dhaka. Inputs and fitting labels use complete eligible months no later than target minus two months, with known publication timestamps no later than issue. Latest eligible donor must be target minus two or minus three calendar months; otherwise abstain. This permits reporting latency while predicting the entire next month before it begins. Unknown historical publication times are assumed and counted; current canonical revisions make this a retrospective scenario, not verified point-in-time performance. Historical exact issue possession is not asserted from later retrieval timestamps.

Baselines: (1) latest eligible donor's monthly total unchanged; (2) donor daily mean multiplied by target calendar days. Learned models: training-only StandardScaler + OLS and Ridge(alpha=1), identical features [donor daily mean BDT, target-month sine, target-month cosine]; target is monthly mean daily cost, prediction multiplied by known target day count. Fixed settings, no hyperparameter tuning, clipping or post-score selection. Primary learned model: Ridge, fixed before scoring. Eight usable chronological fit rows minimum for either learner. Each historical target refits only on eligible labels whose report month is no later than target minus two and meets known publication cutoffs. Training feature origin is each training target's own historical issue, never the later outer origin. No target from a future month enters a fit.

Report every calendar target, original target eligibility, issue, donor month/age, all model forecasts or abstention reason, fitting row/month identities and model/scaler coefficients. Score MAE, RMSE and prediction-minus-actual bias in BDT and million BDT; percentage errors only positive actuals. Report own-date and all-four shared-date errors, coverage and per-month actual/prediction/error. Compare primary Ridge versus day-adjusted baseline on identical months; fewer than six shared scored months is insufficient for a performance conclusion. Even six months is a small exploratory sample, not confirmatory evidence. No p-values or calibrated intervals.

Prospective output: retain actual capture before actual issue, exact input snapshot and model identity. Use same fixed methods and eligibility. Forecast next calendar month at actual issue time only when target has not started. Fit through latest complete eligible month available by capture, retaining unknown official publication flags. This issue can be earlier/later than historical day-eight noon; disclose timing difference. Never backdate to the planned time or call an unavailable outcome zero error. No ongoing scheduler or production forecast replacement.

Feasibility refusal is an experiment outcome: publish precise missing dates/months and available sample if targets or fit rows are insufficient. Do not lower the eight-row floor or loosen completeness after seeing results. If fewer than eight rows support future learners, publish baseline-only forecasts when possible and no learned prediction. The already evaluated 2025 energy result remains untouched; this monthly-cost study is separate and exploratory, with no newly untouched historical final test.
